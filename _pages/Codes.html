I develop research software to translate geometric and mechanical formulations into reproducible computational workflows. Public codes are linked below where available. Please cite the associated paper(s) when using them for research or industrial work.

RUC model — local mechanical analysis of submarine power cables

A repeated-unit-cell workflow for efficient local analysis of submarine power cables. The package includes FORTRAN routines for periodic boundary conditions and Python tools used in preprocessing helical beam/shell representations.

Associated papers:
Fang, P. et al. (2024), Development of an effective modeling method for the mechanical analysis of three-core submarine power cables under tension, Engineering Structures 317, 118632. Paper
Fang, P. et al. (2023), Bending study of submarine power cables based on a repeated unit cell model, Engineering Structures 293, 116606. Paper

Download code (.zip)

HBFM — Hierarchical Bishop-Frame Mapping

A geometry framework under active development for recursive hierarchical helical structures. It uses rotation-minimising frames to separate carrier transport from orbital/material phase and is being developed for geometry generation, topology-preserving modelling, finite-element projection, and fabrication workflows.

Status: research code under active development; public release will follow the associated publication and documentation.

Thermo-mechanical material routines for cable bending

FORTRAN user-material routines developed for bending analysis of submarine power cables with temperature-dependent material response and coupled thermo-electro-mechanical effects.

Status: supporting research code; release and documentation are being consolidated.
