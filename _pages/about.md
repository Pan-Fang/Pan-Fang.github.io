My research focuses on the **mechanics of slender and hierarchical structures**. I combine geometric mechanics, computational mechanics, contact and instability analysis, and multiscale modelling to understand how complex filamentary and helical systems are formed, interact, and carry load. Applications include marine cables, ropes, flexible structures, and emerging architected structures.

I am currently a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Ghent University**. My present work develops geometry-to-mechanics frameworks for hierarchical helical systems, with particular interest in rotation-minimising (Bishop) frames, recursive geometry, contact-driven mechanical response, instability and bifurcation, and physical validation using additive manufacturing and tomography. A central goal is to connect accurate geometric representation with reliable mechanical prediction.

I received my PhD from the Faculty of **Mechanical, Maritime and Materials Engineering (3mE)** at **Delft University of Technology (TU Delft)**, supervised by Prof. [Hans Hopman](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/j.j.hopman/?cHash=2bb7c806023d588d9fe61e6db2281977) and Dr. [Xiaoli Jiang](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/x.jiang/). My doctoral research developed efficient multiscale methods for the local mechanical analysis of submarine power cables, including repeated-unit-cell modelling, interface contact and stick-slip, residual contact stress, and coupled thermo-electro-mechanical analysis.

I received my master's degree from the College of Civil Engineering at **Zhejiang University** in 2019, supervised by Prof. [Yong Bai](https://person.zju.edu.cn/en/0010294#0). My work covered flexible-pipe mechanics, composite failure, torsion, and interface stick-slip.

Research interests
======

- Mechanics of slender and hierarchical structures
- Geometric and computational mechanics
- Helical structures, contact, friction, and instability
- Multiscale modelling and homogenisation
- Marine cables, ropes, and flexible structures
- Additive manufacturing and tomography for mechanics validation
